What is the SAT exam?

Overview

FAQ:

What are the topics coming in the SAT?

The contents of the syllabus are Reading and Writing along with Math. The Reading and Writing Section contains comprehension, Grammer and punctuation questions, whilst the Math section tests the candidates ability on algebra, expressions, graphs and etc.

How many times is the SATs offered?

The SATs are avaliable around 7 to 8 times per year, typically on Tuesdays

What is the SATs score range?

The SATs are based on a scale from up to 1600 in total. 200-800 points for Reading and Writing, same as the Math section.
